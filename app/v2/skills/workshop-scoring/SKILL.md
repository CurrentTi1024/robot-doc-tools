# Scoring

Apply the supplied criterion and its rating bands. Check the conditions against the actual text, tables, native flow connections and images. Preserve the distinction between a target, an estimate and a measured result. For quantitative claims, verify units and the basis of comparison. Use the calculation tool when the rubric provides a formula and its inputs are available.

Choose the supported band first, then a score inside that band. Explain only findings supported by inspected evidence IDs. If the evidence is too unclear or contradictory to score, use `uncertain`. Use `missing` only after checking the eligible evidence and finding no support. The service applies any explicitly configured missing-evidence score. Flag material factual conflicts for human review.
